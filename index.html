How to Play

Goal
Cross all four chalk lines and make it back home to score. First to 2 of 3 loops wins.
Guards
Stay on your chalk line, or the center spine as Patotot. Lunging off your line stuns you for 2 seconds.
Kulong
Tagged runners go to jail until a teammate finishes a lap. If all 5 get jailed, sides swap.
Stamina
Running drains energy and skills cost 12 to 24. Stand still or use Palamig to recover.
Hazards
Watch for passing tricycles, stray dogs, and wet puddles cutting across the street.
Modes
Play vs bots, 5v5 with friends, or 1v1 Duel where Tab swaps between all 5 kids.
Controls

WASD Move · Space Tag · Q / E Skills · Tab Swap · V Scoreboard (Touch supported on mobile)
